Supplier invoices: how to

The tool reads the invoice for you. You answer a few questions and upload the files. If anything goes wrong, contact Zein.

1. Scan

  1. Scan the invoice. All pages of one invoice go in one PDF.
  2. Save the PDF in the input folder (in the invoice tool folder). Several invoices at once is fine.
Not for the tool: handwritten invoices (ED Sport, Aristidou water) and Asian Food Market. Enter those by hand, as before.

2. Run

  1. Double-click Process Invoices (on the desktop). Wait while it reads the invoices.
  2. Answer each question. Use the paper invoice, and the product itself when it asks for a barcode or how many pieces.

3. Answering questions

  • "1 unit = 24 pieces?" Check the box or the shelf. Press YES or NO and type the right number.
  • "Is it this item?" The words that differ are in red. Look at them carefully (A2 vs A3, Black vs Blue).
  • "This item was not found": scan the barcode on one piece, or search by name. Only press It is a NEW item if we really don't sell it yet.
  • New item form:
    • Name: the item as we sell it (one piece, unless we sell the pack).
    • Barcode: scan it from the item itself, not from the invoice.
    • If a green Use this one button appears on the right, we already sell it: press it instead of making a new item.
    • The tool works out the cost. It refuses a selling price below cost: check the price and "how many".
  • Pressed the wrong button? Press < Previous question.
  • Not sure? Press I'm not sure. The invoice goes to Zein. Do not upload it.
  • The window is too small? Scroll down (mouse wheel) to see the rest.

4. Upload to PowerSoft

The Done screen tells you exactly what to upload. Follow its steps in order:
  1. Items import, with Modify ticked: only the files it lists (new items or new costs).
  2. Purchase order: the files in Ready to upload.
Press Open 'Ready to upload' to find the files.
  • Rows in red are new items: check the name, barcode and price before uploading.
  • Never create items by hand in PowerSoft. New items only come through the tool.
  • Never upload a file listed under "Waiting for Zein".

If something goes wrong

If the tool shows "contact Zein", send Zein a message (a photo of the screen helps). The details are saved on the PC for him. You can close the tool at any time: your answers are kept and the questions come back next time.
